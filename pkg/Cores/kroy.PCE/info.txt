Core by Gregory Estrade, srg320, and greyrogue. Pocket port by agg23. Accuracy
fixes tracking MiSTer by vanfanel. Cheat support by kroy.

PC Engine (PCE), known as TurboGrafx-16 in the United States, was created by NEC
and was the first 4th generation console to reach the market. Therefore, even
though it came out in the late 80s, it has significantly better graphics than
8-bit systems that were being sold at the time, such as the NES.

This is a fork that adds cheat support. Every published PC Engine cheat is a
work RAM poke, so cheats are applied by writing values into memory once per
frame rather than by patching ROM reads. Turn them on with "Cheats Enabled" in
the core menu.

Differences from the upstream agg23 port:

  - Cheats.
  - PC Engine CD support from cue plus bin, including CD audio and cue-named
    backup RAM saves.
  - SuperGrafx is removed to make room for cheats and CD. .sgx files still
    load, but they render as plain PC Engine and will not look right.
  - "Swap ROM Bit Order" for US TurboChip dumps taken bit-reversed.

Loads standard PC Engine and TurboGrafx ROMs from the SD card. CD games use a
single-bin cue plus its bin and require a System Card image in
Assets/pce/common. CHD and bare ISO files are not supported.

Report issues with this build to kroy, not to agg23 or vanfanel.
